# FIRST CONTACT

- If your life had a soundtrack, what song would play the moment you walked into a room?
- What's a completely useless skill you're weirdly proud of?
- If you had to give a TED talk tomorrow with zero prep, what topic could you actually pull off?
- What's the most "you" purchase you've ever made — the one that made a friend go "yep, that tracks"?
- If your childhood self saw you right now, what's the first thing they'd ask about?
- What's a food combination you love that other people find suspicious?
- If you could instantly become fluent in one skill overnight, no effort required, what would you pick — and be honest about why.
