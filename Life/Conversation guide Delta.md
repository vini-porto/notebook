# FIRST CONTACT

- If your life had a soundtrack, what song would play the moment you walked into a room?
- What's a completely useless skill you're weirdly proud of?
- If you had to give a TED talk tomorrow with zero prep, what topic could you actually pull off?
- What's the most "you" purchase you've ever made — the one that made a friend go "yep, that tracks"?
- If your childhood self saw you right now, what's the first thing they'd ask about?
- What's a food combination you love that other people find suspicious?
- If you could instantly become fluent in one skill overnight, no effort required, what would you pick — and be honest about why.

# GETTING ACQUAINTED

- What's something you believed with total confidence as a kid that turned out to be completely wrong?
- What's a small, ordinary moment from the last week that made you unreasonably happy?
- If you had to describe your sense of humor as a weather pattern, what would it be?
- What's a rule you break on purpose because you think it's dumb?
- Who's someone — famous or not — whose way of thinking you low-key study?
- What's a hill you'll die on that most people think is a weird thing to care about?
- If your friends had to sell you to a stranger in one sentence, what would they say?

# BUILDING RAPPORT

- What's something you used to be embarrassed about that you now genuinely love about yourself?
- If you could mail a letter to yourself five years from now, what's one thing you'd want to know had happened?
- What's a belief you held strongly and later completely reversed?
- What's something you do that you'd never admit to on a first date, but will admit to right now?
- If you woke up tomorrow with enough money that work was optional, what's the first thing that would actually change about your day?
- What's a compliment you received once that you still think about?
- What's something small you do for other people that you never tell anyone about?

# GROWING TRUST

- What's a moment you look back on where you surprised yourself with how strong you were?
- What's something you're quietly still working through, even if it doesn't show?
- Is there a version of yourself from a few years ago you'd want to sit down and reassure? What would you tell them?
- What's a fear you've never said out loud to someone new this early?
- What's something people misread about you almost immediately, and how do you wish they saw it instead?
- When do you feel most like yourself — and when do you feel like you're performing a version of yourself?
- What's a piece of advice you needed at some point but nobody gave you?

# NEAR-INTIMATE

- What does being truly known by someone actually feel like to you — not loved, known?
- What's a moment you felt completely safe with another person, and what made it feel that way?
- Is there something you've never said to someone you loved, that you wish you had?
- What's a part of your identity that took you the longest to accept?
- When you imagine your future, is there a person in it you haven't met yet — what do you hope they're like?
- What's something you need from the people close to you that you rarely ask for directly?
- What would it mean to you if someone remembered the small things you say without you ever repeating them?

# DEEP INTIMACY

- What's something you're afraid to want, because wanting it makes it possible to lose?
- If you knew this conversation was the last real one you'd have with someone, what would you make sure they understood about you?
- What's a grief — big or small — that shaped who you are, that most people don't know about?
- What does it feel like in your body when you finally let your guard down around someone?
- Is there a version of love you're afraid you don't deserve? Where do you think that came from?
- What's something about existing — just being alive right now — that quietly terrifies or amazes you?
- If someone loved every part of you, including the parts you hide, what do you imagine that would actually feel like?
