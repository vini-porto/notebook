What is globalization?	A process and condition involving the increasing interconnectedness of different parts of the world through common processes of economic, environmental, political and cultural change.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What does globalization challenge, according to the lecture?	Conventional state sovereignty and human populations.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is a transnational corporation (TNC)?	A corporation registered and operating in more than one country at a time, with headquarters in one country and wholly or partially owned subsidiaries in others. Also called a multinational corporation (MNC).	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is a non-governmental organization (NGO)?	A voluntary group, usually not affiliated with any government, formed to provide services or advocate a public policy; most are non-profit.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
Give three NGO examples from the lecture.	Red Cross/Red Crescent, Greenpeace, Human Rights Watch.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
When does the world system begin, and what drove it?	In the late 1400s, driven by economic gain through the European voyages of discovery.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
Name two factors that led to the beginnings of the world system.	Advances in shipbuilding and the replacement of feudalism with merchant capitalism.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What was feudalism?	A system in which people were given land and protection by people of higher rank and worked and fought for them in return.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
Which five countries built empires between the 15th and mid 18th centuries?	England, France, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
Name the first multinational corporations and their founding years.	British East India Company (1600), Dutch East India Company (1602), Hudson's Bay Company (1670).	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is colonialism?	The establishment and maintenance of political, economic and legal domination over a separate and foreign society (land and people).	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is imperialism?	State policy, practice or advocacy of extending power, especially by direct territorial acquisition or gaining political and economic control of other areas.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is decolonization?	The process by which colonies become independent of the colonizing country; the return of sovereignty to original inhabitants.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
By when was decolonization almost complete?	By the 1960s and 1970s.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is neocolonialism?	Control of less-developed countries by developed countries through indirect means (TNCs, foreign aid, IGOs, etc.).	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
In 2016, how many of the world's top 100 economies were corporations?	69 (the other 31 were countries).	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What was NAFTA?	A 1994 agreement between Canada, the US and Mexico that eliminated tariffs and other trade barriers between them.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is a tariff?	A tax on imports.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is the ninth attribute of globalization?	Economic disparities between rich and poor regions that drive people to migrate, legally and illegally, in search of a better life.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
In which direction does most immigration happen?	From poorer countries to richer ones.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What do proponents say economic globalization brings?	Efficiency, lower prices, higher profits and diversity of products.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What do critics say economic globalization brings?	Widespread inequality and homogeneity.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is a sweatshop?	A manufacturing facility where workers are ill-treated, typically confined in crowded conditions for long hours at very low pay.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is outsourcing?	A strategy, common since the 1980s, where corporations in developed nations move non-management operations to places where land and labour are cheap and regulation is low.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is "brain drain"?	When the most educated individuals are recruited to work abroad.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
What is economic convergence?	The idea that poorer countries will eventually "catch up" to richer ones.	GEN100 week-04 global-citizenship
