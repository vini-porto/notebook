# bee's knees
#Noun: o máximo / o melhor
- (excellence) excellent or ideally suitable person or thing #dated #UK 
---
- ==Her new dress is the bee's knees.==
- ==This gadget is the bee's knees for tech lovers.==

# bust someone's bubble
#Noun: PT: acabar com a alegria de alguém / quebrar a ilusäo de alguém
- (disappointment) ruin someone's happy moment #US 
- (emotion) disappoint someone by revealing the truth #US
---
- ==Don't bust her bubble with bad news.==
- ==Sorry to bust your bubble, but that's not true.==
- ==I don't want to bust your bubble, but we can't go.==
