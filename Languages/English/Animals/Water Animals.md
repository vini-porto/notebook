## Mammals

### Whale
![[Pasted image 20260809235031.png|305]]

>[!EXAMPLE] Expressions with **Whale**
>- **whale away:** hit something repeatedly and forcefully
>	- The boxer whaled away at his opponent.
>- **whale on**: criticize or scold harshly 
>	- The coach would often whale on the players for their mistakes.
>	- Parents shouldn't whale on their kids for small errors.
>- **a whale of a:** extremely good or enjoyable / very large or impressive
> 	 - We had a whale of a time at the amusement park.
> 	 - They had a whale of a party last night.
> - **whale of a time:** extremely good time
> 	  - She had a whale of a time at the concert.
> 	- The kids had a whale of a time at the amusement park.
