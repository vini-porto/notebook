### Cod
*(marine) type of fish found in colder waters*
*(verb: [[to cod]]) joke or kid around with someone*

![[Pasted image 20260808234145.png|261]]

- ==**Cod** can be found in many Atlantic fisheries.==
- ==His story was just a **cod** to lighten the mood.==
- ==Everyone laughed at the **cod** he pulled during the meeting.==
- ==He likes to **cod** with his friends during lunch.==
- ==She would often **cod** about her cooking skills.==
- ==She tried to **cod** her brother with a fake spider.==
