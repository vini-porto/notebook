# Potato
#Noun:
- (food) starchy tuber eaten as a vegetable
- (technology) underpowered computer or device

---
>[!note] **PT:**  
>- Batata

---

>[!EXAMPLE] Expressions with **Potato**
>- **hot potato:** (games) game where players pass an object quickly / (controversy) controversial issue or situation that is awkward or
unpleasant to deal with
>	>[!note]- **PT:** jogo da batata quente / questdo controversa
>	- ==The kids played hot potato at the party.==
>	- ==The debate about gun control is a real hot potato in US politics.==
>- **couch potato:** (TV viewer) person who spends much time sitting or lying watching television #US 
>	>[!note]- **PT:** preguicoso / viciado em TV
>	- ==He became a couch potato during the long winter months.==
>	- ==She became a couch potato after losing her job.==
>- **mouse potato:** (computer user) person who spends a lot of time using a computer
>	>[!note] **PT:** viciado em computador
>	- ==He's a real mouse potato, always playing games online.==
>- **drop like a hot potato:** (figurative) stop supporting or being involved with something or someone very suddenly #US 
>	>[!note] **PT:** largarde mão / pular fora
>	- ==Sponsors will drop like a hot potato if the scandal grows.==
>- **political hot potato:** (public debate) matter that sparks quarrels and officials often dodge
>	>[!note] **PT:** tema polémico / questdo polémica
>	- ==Immigration reform remains a political hot potato during election season.==

- ==A **potato** can be boiled, fried, or roasted.==
- ==This phone is a **potato** and can't handle new apps.==
- ==My old tablet is such a **potato**, it crashes often.==
