# Cabbage
#Noun: 
- (food) edible leafy head vegetable formed by tightly packed leaves. ***PT: Repolho***
- (finance) money in slang terms. ***PT: Grana***
- (affection) used as a term of endearment. ***PT: Querido/ Chuchu***
- (insulting term) person with severely reduced mental capacities. ***PT: Incapacitado / Vegetal*** #Rude
#Verb:
- (idling) do nothing or idle around. ***PT: Vadiar/Procastinar*** #UK 
- (stealing) steal or pilfer something.  ***PT: Roubar/Furtar*** #UK #Slang #rare
