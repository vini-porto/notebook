# bring up
*to start discussing something*
- ==There is something I want to **bring up**.==
- ==Let's **bring** this **up** at our next team meeting.==

# get across
*Often used with try when you want to help someone understand something*

- ==Am I **getting** the message across clearly?==
- ==I'm trying to **get** my point **across** but Paul keeps **butting in**!==

# jump in
*great for interrupting*

- ==Do you mind if I **jump in** here?==
	- **Jump in** is just an informal synonym of **interrupt**
# butt in
*used for interrupting, very informal*

- ==I was speaking with Sue after she lost her job; then Jim **butts in** and starts talking about his new promotion.==

# blurt out
*to say something you're not supposed to say*

- ==I'm so sorry! | just **blurted** it **out**!==

# get through to
1. When you're trying to call someone and they don't answer.
	- ==I'm trying to get through but there's no answer.==
2. when you are trying to make someone understand what you are trying to say.
	- ==It's difficult to **get** the message **through** to my team because we all work remotely.==
	- ==I'm trying to **get** the message **through** to Paul but he's not listening.==
# Talk (sth.) over
*to discuss a problem or a plan*
- ==I know you're upset; let's **talk** it **over** tonight when you get home.==

*to speak when someone else is speaking; to intentionally interrupt them*
- ==I don't mean to **talk** **over** you, but we have already decided what to do.==

# talk (so.) around
to convince or persuade someone to agree.

- ==I didn't want to go, but he succeeded in talking me around.==

# shut (so.) down
*informally used during conversations when you get rejected*

- ==It was kind of awkward - She just **shut** him **down** mid-sentence.==
- ==My boss didn't like the suggestion. He **shut** me **down** straight away.==

# back (so.) up
*to give support by telling other people you agree with something they said/did.*

- ==Thanks for backing me up during the meeting.==
